Why a Curator.

A good wine list is a series of decisions, and most of them are made badly under time pressure. Too many bottles, chosen to be safe, poured in the wrong order.

We taste constantly and we hold opinions. What you get is a short list with a reason behind every bottle — fewer choices, better ones, and no performance required of you.

How It Works.

The Brief

One conversation. The occasion, the room, the budget, the constraints — allergies, dry guests, a cellar you already own.

The Dossier

You receive a document, not a spreadsheet. Every bottle named, priced and sourced, each one with the story of why it's on your table — the grower, the vintage, what it does next to the food — written to be said out loud, not recited.

The Table

Serving order, temperatures, glassware, what to open if the night runs long. Standing clients get the next rotation before they ask.

Who This Is For.

Collectors building standing relationships. Events that need to feel intentional. Corporate dinners where wine sets the tone. Anyone who knows that the right bottles change the room.
